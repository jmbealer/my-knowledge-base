# Culture and the Arts

[Home Index](../0-home-idx.md) | [Culture and the Arts Index](./00-cult-art-idx.md)

## Overview

This portal covers the social behavior and norms of human societies (Culture) and the physical expression of creativity (The Arts).

## What is Culture?

Culture is the social behavior, institutions, and norms found in human societies, as well as the knowledge, beliefs, arts, laws, customs, capabilities, and habits of the individuals in these groups.

### Examples

- Traditions and customs.
- Language and communication.
- Cuisine and foodways.
- Social norms and values.

## What is the Arts?

The arts refer to the theory and physical expression of creativity found in human cultures and societies through skills and imagination.

### Examples

- Visual Arts (Painting, Sculpture).
- Literary Arts (Poetry, Prose).
- Performing Arts (Music, Dance, Theater).
- Decorative Arts.
