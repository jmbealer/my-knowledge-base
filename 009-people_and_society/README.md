# People and Society

[Home Index](../0-home-idx.md) | [People and Society Index](./00-peo-soc-idx.md)

## What is People?

"People" refers to a plurality of human beings considered as a whole, often sharing a common culture, language, or territory.

### Examples

- Biographies and historical figures.
- Ethnic groups and demographics.

## What is Society?

A society is a group of individuals involved in persistent social interaction sharing the same spatial or social territory.

### Examples

- Social institutions (Schools, Governments).
- Communities and social structures.
- Economics and systems of production.
