# People and Society

## What is People?
"People" refers to a plurality of human beings considered as a whole, often sharing a common culture, language, or territory. In a more individual sense, it refers to the persons who make up a community or nation.

### Examples of People
- **Individual Biographies**: The lives and contributions of specific historical or contemporary figures.
- **Ethnic Groups**: Communities of people who share common ancestry, language, or cultural traditions.
- **Demographics**: The statistical study of human populations (e.g., age, gender, income).

## What is Society?
A society is a group of individuals involved in persistent social interaction, or a large social group sharing the same spatial or social territory, typically subject to the same political authority and dominant cultural expectations.

### Examples of Society
- **Institutions**: Established organizations or foundations, such as schools, hospitals, and governments.
- **Communities**: Groups of people living in the same place or having a particular characteristic in common.
- **Social Structures**: The stable arrangement of institutions and social patterns that determine the relationships between individuals in a society.
- **Economics**: The system by which a society organizes the production, distribution, and consumption of goods and services.
