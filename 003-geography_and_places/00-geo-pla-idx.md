# Geography and Places

## What is Geography?

Geography is the study of places and the relationships between people and their environments. Geographers explore both the physical properties of Earth's surface and the human societies spread across it.

### Examples of Geography

- **Physical Geography**: The study of natural features like mountains, rivers, and climate.
- **Human Geography**: The study of human communities, cultures, and their interactions with the environment.
- **Cartography**: The art and science of making maps.
- **Geomorphology**: The study of landforms and the processes that shape them.

## What is Places?

In geography, a place is a specific point on Earth's surface that has been given meaning by human experience. It can be a city, a country, a building, or even a sacred site.

### Examples of Places

- **Natural Landmarks**: Mount Everest, the Grand Canyon, or the Amazon Rainforest.
- **Human Settlements**: Cities like Tokyo, villages, or metropolitan areas.
- **Political Entities**: Countries, states, and provinces.
- **Cultural Sites**: The Great Wall of China, the Pyramids of Giza, or local community centers.
