# Geography and Places

[Home Index](../0-home-idx.md) | [Geography and Places Index](./00-geo-pla-idx.md)

## What is Geography?

Geography is the study of places and the relationships between people and their environments. It explores both the physical properties of Earth's surface and the human societies spread across it.

### Examples

- Physical Geography (Landforms, Climate).
- Human Geography (Communities, Cultures).
- Cartography (Map making).

## What is Places?

In geography, a place is a specific point on Earth's surface that has been given meaning by human experience.

### Examples

- Natural Landmarks.
- Human Settlements (Cities, Towns).
- Political Entities (Countries, States).
