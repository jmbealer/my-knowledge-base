# Society and Social Sciences

## What is Society?
Society is a group of individuals involved in persistent social interaction, or a large social group sharing the same spatial or social territory, typically subject to the same political authority and dominant cultural expectations.

### Examples of Society
- **Social Institutions**: Family, education, government, and economy.
- **Culture**: The shared beliefs, values, and practices of a group of people.
- **Social Stratification**: The hierarchical arrangement of individuals into social classes, castes, or tiers.

## What is Social Science?
Social science is the branch of science devoted to the study of societies and the relationships among individuals within those societies. It covers a broad range of disciplines that examine human behavior and social patterns.

### Examples of Social Science
- **Psychology**: The study of the human mind and behavior.
- **Sociology**: The study of social life, social change, and the social causes and consequences of human behavior.
- **Economics**: The study of how people allocate scarce resources for production, distribution, and consumption.
- **Political Science**: The study of systems of government and the analysis of political activities, thoughts, and behavior.
- **Anthropology**: The study of humans, human behavior, and societies in the past and present.
