## What is Religion?
Religion is a social-cultural system of designated behaviors and practices, worldviews, texts, and sanctified places that relates humanity to spiritual elements.

### Examples
- Abrahamic and Indian religions.
- Indigenous and East Asian belief systems.

## What is a Belief System?
A belief system is a set of mutually supportive beliefs, which can be religious, philosophical, or ideological.

### Examples
- Philosophical systems (Stoicism, Humanism).
- Political ideologies and Mythology.
- Secularism and cultural worldviews.
