# Natural and Physical Sciences

## What is Natural Science?
Natural science is a branch of science that deals with the physical world, e.g., physics, chemistry, geology, and biology. It uses empirical evidence from observation and experimentation to understand the laws of nature.

### Examples of Natural Science
- **Biology**: The study of life and living organisms.
- **Earth Science**: The study of the Earth and its neighbors in space (e.g., geology, meteorology).
- **Life Sciences**: Branches of science that involve the scientific study of organisms (e.g., zoology, botany).

## What is Physical Science?
Physical science is the study of inanimate natural objects and the laws that govern them. It includes physics, chemistry, astronomy, and Earth sciences.

### Examples of Physical Science
- **Physics**: The study of matter, energy, and the fundamental forces of nature.
- **Chemistry**: The study of substances and the changes they undergo.
- **Astronomy**: The study of celestial objects and the universe as a whole.

## What is the difference between Natural and Physical Sciences?
While "Natural Science" is a broad term encompassing everything in the natural world (including living things), "Physical Science" specifically focuses on non-living systems. In many contexts, physical science is considered a major branch of natural science, alongside life science.
