## What is Natural Science?
Natural science is a branch of science that deals with the physical world, using empirical evidence from observation and experimentation.

### Examples
- Biology (Life).
- Earth Science (Geology, Meteorology).

## What is Physical Science?
Physical science is the study of inanimate natural objects and the laws that govern them.

### Examples
- Physics (Matter, Energy).
- Chemistry (Substances).
- Astronomy (Celestial objects).
