## What is Philosophy?
Philosophy is the systematic study of general and fundamental questions about existence, reason, knowledge, and values.

### Examples
- Ethics and Morality.
- Metaphysics and Reality.
- Epistemology and Knowledge.

## What is Thinking?
Thinking is the mental process in which beings form psychological associations and models of the world.

### Examples
- Critical and Creative thinking.
- Logical reasoning and Decision making.
