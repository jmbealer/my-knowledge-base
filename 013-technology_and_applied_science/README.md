# Technology and Applied Science

[Home Index](../0-home-idx.md) | [Technology and Applied Science Index](./00-tech-applied-idx.md)

## What is Technology?

Technology is the sum of any techniques, skills, methods, and processes used in the production of goods or services or in the accomplishment of objectives.

### Examples

- Information Technology and Engineering.
- Biotechnology and Nanotechnology.

## What is Applied Science?

Applied science is the use of the scientific method and knowledge to attain practical goals.

### Examples

- Computer Science and Automation.
- Medicine and Diagnostic sciences.
- Material Science.
