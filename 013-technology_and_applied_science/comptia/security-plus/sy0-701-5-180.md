# 5.0 Security Program Management and Oversight

## 5.1 Summarize elements of effective security governance

**Guidelines**

**Policies:** High-level strategic statements of intent (the "What" and "Why").

- **Acceptable Use Policy (AUP):** Defines appropriate and prohibited use of company assets.
- **Information Security Policies:** The master list of rules for protecting CIA.
- **Business continuity**
- **Disaster recovery**
- **Incident response**
- **Software development lifecycle (SDLC)**
- **SDLC Policy:** Guides the secure development of software from requirements to maintenance.
- **Change Management Policy:** Ensures modifications are structured and approved.

**Standards:** Mandatory rules or actions that support policies (e.g., "All passwords must be 14 characters").

- **Password**
- **Access control**
- **Physical security**
- **Encryption**

**Procedures**
**Procedures:** Step-by-step instructions for specific tasks (Onboarding, Change Control, Incident Response).

- **Change management**
- **Onboarding/oboarding**
- **Playbooks:** Conditional, step-by-step guides for specific incident scenarios (e.g., Ransomware recovery).

**External considerations**

- **Regulatory**
- **Legal**
- **Industry**
- **Local/regional**
- **National**
- **Global**

**Monitoring and revision**

**Types of governance structures**

- **Boards:** Specialized panels that gather info and oversee management.
- **Committees:** Primary decision-makers for specific focus areas (e.g., Audit Committee).
- **Government entities**
- **Centralized/decentralized**
- **Centralized vs. Decentralized:** Centralized offers consistent authority; decentralized offers local responsiveness.

**Roles and responsibilities for systems and data**

- **Owners**
- **Data Owner:** Accountable for the data (usually a senior officer/executive).
- **Controllers**
- **Data Controller:** Determines the purpose and means of processing personal data.
- **Processors**
- **Data Processor:** Processes data on behalf of the controller (often a 3rd party like a payroll company).
- **Custodians/stewards**
- **Data Custodian/Steward:** Handles technical implementation, security controls, and accuracy.

## 5.2 Explain elements of the risk management process

**Risk identification**
**Risk assessment**

- **Ad hoc:** Conducted as needed in response to specific events.
- **Recurring:** Scheduled intervals (Quarterly/Annual).
- **One-time**
- **Continuous:** Real-time monitoring and evaluation.

**Risk analysis**

- **Qualitative:** Subjective ranking (High, Medium, Low) based on experience and impact/likelihood matrices.
- **Quantitative:** Objective, numerical evaluation using financial metrics:
- **Single Loss Expectancy (SLE):** Loss from one event ($) = Asset Value x Exposure Factor (EF).
- **Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE):** Total annual risk cost ($) = SLE x ARO.
- **Annualized Rate of Occurrence (ARO):** How many times the event happens per year.
- **Probability**
- **Likelihood**
- **Exposure factor**
- **Impact**

**Risk register**

- **Key risk indicators**
- **Risk owners**
- **Risk threshold**

**Risk tolerance**

**Risk appetite**

- **Expansionary**
- **Conservative**
- **Neutral**

**Risk management strategies**

- **Transfer:** Moving risk to a 3rd party (Cyber Insurance).
- **Accept:** Choosing to live with the risk.
  - **Exemption:** Being excluded from a rule.
  - **Exception:** Bypassing a policy for a specific, justified condition.
- **Avoid:** Stopping the activity that causes the risk.
- **Mitigate:** Implementing controls to reduce likelihood or impact.

**Risk reporting**

**Business impact analysis**

- **RTO (Recovery Time Objective):** Target time for restoring a business process.
- **RPO (Recovery Point Objective):** Maximum acceptable data loss measured in time.
- **MTTR (Mean Time to Repair):** Average time to fix a failure.
- **MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures):** Measure of system reliability.

## 5.3 Explain the processes associated with third-party risk assessment and management

**Vendor assessment**

- **Penetration testing**
- **Right-to-audit clause**
- **Right-to-Audit:** Contractual clause allowing the organization to inspect the vendor's security controls.
- **Evidence of internal audits**
- **Independent assessments**
- **Supply chain analysis**
- **Supply Chain Analysis:** Tracing the origins of components to ensure integrity and avoid tampering.

**Vendor selection**

- **Due Diligence:** Thoroughly vetting a vendor's financial and security health before a partnership.
- **Conflict of interest**

**Agreement types**

- **SLA (Service Level Agreement):** Defines uptime, response times, and penalties for failure.
- **MOA (Memorandum of Agreement):** A step above MOU; conditionally agrees to objectives.
- **MOU (Memorandum of Understanding):** Informal letter of intent; non-binding but documents common goals.
- **MSA (Master Service Agreement):** Broad framework for recurring future transactions.
- **SOW (Statement of Work):** Specific details, deliverables, and timelines for a particular project.
- **NDA (Non-Disclosure Agreement):** Legal contract protecting confidential information.
- **BPA (Business Partners Agreement):** Details ownership, profit-sharing, and decision-making between partners.

## 5.5 Explain types and purposes of audits and assessments

**Attestation**
**Internal**

- **Internal Audits:** Conducted by the organization's own team to validate controls and prepare for external audits.

- **Compliance**
- **Audit committee**
- **Self-assessments**

**External**

- **External Audits:** Independent evaluations by 3rd parties (often required for regulatory compliance).

- **Regulatory**
- **Examinations**
- **Assessment**
- **Independent third-party audit**

**Penetration testing**

- **Physical**
- **Offensive**
- **Defensive**
- **Integrated**
- **Known environment**
  - **Known:** Full access to documentation (White Box).
- **Partially known environment**
  - **Partially Known:** Limited info (Gray Box).
- **Unknown environment**
  - **Unknown:** No info provided; simulates external attacker (Black Box).
- **Reconnaissance**
  - **Passive:** OSINT, social media, no direct interaction with target systems.
  - **Active:** Port scanning, ping sweeps, direct probing.
