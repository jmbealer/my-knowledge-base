# History and Events

## What is History?

History is the systematic study and documentation of the human past. It involves the analysis of events, people, and societies over time to understand how the world has changed and how the present came to be.

### Examples of History

- **Ancient History**: The study of civilizations like Egypt, Greece, and Rome.
- **Modern History**: The study of world events from the late 15th century to the present.
- **Oral History**: The collection and study of historical information using sound recordings of interviews with people having personal knowledge of past events.
- **Historiography**: The study of the methods of historians in developing history as an academic discipline.

## What is an Event?

An event is a specific occurrence or happening of significance that takes place at a particular time and location. In a historical context, events are often the building blocks used to construct a narrative of the past.

### Examples of Events

- **Political Events**: Elections, revolutions, and the signing of treaties.
- **Natural Events**: Earthquakes, floods, and other disasters that impact human society.
- **Cultural Events**: Festivals, world fairs, and major artistic movements.
- **Technological Events**: The invention of the printing press or the first moon landing.
