## What is History?

History is the systematic study and documentation of the human past.

### Examples

- Ancient and Modern History.
- Oral History and Historiography.

## What is an Event?

An event is a specific occurrence or happening of significance that takes place at a particular time and location.

### Examples

- Political and Cultural happenings.
- Natural disasters and Technological breakthroughs.
