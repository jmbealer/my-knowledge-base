## 1.6 Compare and contrast various types of network traffic

### Traffic Types
- **Unicast:** One-to-one communication.
- **Broadcast:** One-to-all (IPv4 only). Used for ARP and DHCP Discover.
- **Multicast:** One-to-many (specific group). Range: **224.0.0.0/4**.
- **Anycast:** One-to-nearest (used in IPv6 and DNS).

### Network Planes
- **Control Plane:** Centralized logic that makes decisions about where traffic is sent (Routing tables, STP).
- **Data Plane:** The actual forwarding of packets based on control plane decisions.
