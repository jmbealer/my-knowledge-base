# Mathematics and Logic

## What is Mathematics?
Mathematics is the study of numbers, quantities, shapes, and patterns. It provides a formal language and framework for understanding and describing the world, from the simplest measurements to the most complex scientific theories.

### Examples of Mathematics
- **Arithmetic**: The study of basic numbers and operations like addition and multiplication.
- **Algebra**: The use of symbols and letters to represent numbers and quantities in equations.
- **Geometry**: The study of properties and relations of points, lines, surfaces, and solids.
- **Calculus**: The branch of mathematics that deals with rates of change and accumulation of quantities.

## What is Logic?
Logic is the systematic study of the principles of valid inference and correct reasoning. It involves the analysis of arguments to determine their soundness and consistency.

### Examples of Logic
- **Formal Logic**: The use of symbolic systems to represent and analyze arguments (e.g., propositional logic).
- **Informal Logic**: The analysis of arguments in natural language, often focusing on fallacies.
- **Mathematical Logic**: The application of mathematical techniques to logic.
- **Boolean Logic**: A form of algebra in which all values are reduced to either "true" or "false".
